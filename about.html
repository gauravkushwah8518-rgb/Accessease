Why We Built AccessEase

Traditional web accessibility solutions often require complex server infrastructure, heavy third-party npm dependencies, or expensive monthly SaaS contracts that small creators and businesses cannot afford.

AccessEase was born out of a desire for simplicity and empowerment: a lightweight, lightning-fast toolkit that puts robust accessibility controls directly in the hands of website visitors without compromising site performance or privacy.
